Method & limits

What this page does and does not claim

Constructed, not a benchmark

The 20 claims were written for directional validation. There is no statistical-significance claim and no general-purpose accuracy claim.

Fake citations are hard-rule cases

v0 also caught both fake citations in this run. That was a coincidence of the test set. v1's advantage is deterministic interception and zero LLM tokens for those cases.

Accuracy has a cost

v1.1 improves accuracy from 65% to 85% versus v1, but tokens rise 15.7% and latency rises 1.58s. The page reports both sides.

Public-safe by design

The paper is included as an anonymized Markdown reconstruction. Cover page, author/course metadata, evidence quotes, API keys, and private run logs are not included.
